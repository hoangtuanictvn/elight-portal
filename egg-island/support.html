Common questions

I lost my progress

Progress is stored only on your device. Uninstalling the game or clearing its data removes it, and we cannot restore it because we keep no server backup.

My score is not on the leaderboard

Scores take a few seconds to sync after a run ends. Check your connection and reopen the leaderboard. Scores that look abnormal may be filtered by our anti-cheat checks.

A rewarded ad does not load

This usually depends on your region, your connection or ad availability. Try again in a few minutes. A reward is granted only after the ad provider confirms a completed view.

A purchase did not deliver diamonds

Make sure you are online and reopen the shop. If the diamonds still do not arrive, email us with the date of the purchase and the receipt from your App Store or Google Play order history.

Privacy, tracking and ads

Change ad choices in Settings → Privacy → Ad privacy options. On iPhone, change tracking permission in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. Details are in the privacy policy.
