Egg Island: Merge Eggs

Drop eggs into the basket, merge matching eggs into bigger ones, and raise the cute pets that hatch.

How to play → Support

Drop, merge, hatch.

How to play

When two eggs of the same kind touch, they merge into a bigger one. Keep merging to climb all 12 tiers. Let the basket overflow and the run ends, so choose every drop with care.

Your pet comes along

A pet walks below the basket, collects the gold, gems and food your merges drop, and fills its energy bar when you feed it. A full bar unlocks a skill that clears the board when you are stuck. Three pets to raise, each with levels, evolutions and new skills.

More to do

  • Daily quests and rewards
  • A world leaderboard for high score and pet level
  • Your run is saved when you close the app
