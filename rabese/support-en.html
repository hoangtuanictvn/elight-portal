Frequently asked questions

Is Rabese paid?

Rabese is free. The Android version is supported by ads; the iPhone version has no ads.

Where is my data stored?

Only on your phone. No servers, no cloud sync, no account. See Privacy Policy for details.

Why do some sentences have no built-in audio?

To keep the app a reasonable size, 7,800+ HSK 1 sentences ship with AI-generated pronunciation audio (Kokoro TTS). Other sentences use your phone's Chinese text-to-speech voice. On Android, if no Chinese voice is installed the app offers to install one (system Settings → Text-to-speech).

Some Vietnamese translations look off?

Some translations are machine-generated. We improve based on user feedback. Email us any obvious errors — they'll be fixed in the next update.

How do I delete all learning data?

Go to Settings → Clear learning data. Or uninstall the app.

What languages are supported?

UI: Vietnamese + English. Content: Simplified Chinese with Vietnamese + English translations.
