Elight Studio – Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 9, 2026

This privacy policy applies to the mobile app Elight Studio (the "App"), made by Delight Lab ("we", "us"). It explains what information is collected when you use the App, how it is used and shared, and the choices you have. By installing or using the App you agree to this policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the App and delete it from your device.

1. Summary

  • Your recordings, songs, projects and audio files stay on your device. We never upload or receive them.
  • The App has no user accounts and no analytics.
  • The App shows ads from Google AdMob. Google collects some device information to show and measure ads, as described in section 4.

2. Information we collect

Personal information. We do not collect any personal information, such as your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos or files.

Information on your device. The App works with the following, only on your device:

  • Microphone: used only while you record (in Record, in the Studio, and when you measure recording latency). Recordings are saved on your device.
  • Music and audio files: files you choose are copied into the App's private storage and processed on your device. Results are saved in the Music/ElightLab folder.
  • Songs, MIDI and SoundFonts: Studio projects, notes from a connected MIDI keyboard and SoundFont files you load are stored in the App's private storage.

You can delete this content at any time in the App, with a file manager, or by uninstalling the App.

3. How we use and share information

We do not use, sell, rent or share your personal information or your content. If you choose to share a file with another app (for example a messaging app), that app's privacy policy applies.

4. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The App shows ads served by Google AdMob: a banner on the main screens and, at most once each time you open the App, a full-screen ad after you save something. Ads are never shown while you record or play in the Studio.

To show, limit and measure ads and to prevent fraud, Google's Mobile Ads SDK collects and processes:

  • your device's advertising ID,
  • your IP address and the approximate location derived from it,
  • device information (model, operating system, language, network type) and app information,
  • ad interactions (impressions, clicks) and diagnostic data such as crash logs.

This information is handled by Google under its own policies:

  • How Google uses information from apps that use its services
  • Google Privacy Policy
  • AdMob and privacy

5. Your choices

  • Consent. Where the law requires it (for example in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, Switzerland and some US states), the App asks for your choice before ads are requested. You can change it at any time in the App: Settings, Ad privacy choices.
  • Personalized ads. On Android you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings (Settings, Google, Ads, or Settings, Privacy, Ads). Without it, ads are not personalized.
  • Opt-out tools. You can also manage interest-based advertising at adssettings.google.com and youradchoices.com.

6. Links to other websites and services

Ads in the App may link to third-party websites or apps. If you follow such a link, the information you provide there is governed by that party's privacy policy and terms, not by this policy. We do not control and are not responsible for third-party websites or services.

7. Children

The App is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the App, please contact us and we will help remove it.

8. Security

Your content stays on your device and is protected by your device's security. Data that Google's SDK sends for advertising is encrypted in transit.

9. Legal requirements

We may disclose information if required by law, to respond to legal process, to protect our rights or property, or to investigate fraud, illegal activity or threats to anyone's safety. As we do not collect personal information or your content, we generally have nothing to disclose.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy, for example when the App changes. The new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
