Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 25, 2026

This privacy policy applies to the following mobile apps made by Anh Tuan Hoang, publishing as Elight Labs ("we", "us"):

  • Elight Studio (Android)
  • Slomoly (iPhone and Android)
  • Rabese (iPhone and Android)
  • Maneki Dict (Android)
  • Simple Reader (Android)
  • Egg Island (iPhone and Android)

It explains what information each app handles, how it is used and shared, and the choices you have. Sections 1, 2 and 4–11 apply to all apps; section 3 describes each app.

1. Summary

  • What you create (recordings, songs, videos, learning progress, game progress) stays on your device. We run no servers that receive it.
  • Egg Island is the one exception to the next two points: it has an online leaderboard, uses Firebase analytics and crash reports, offers in-app purchases, and on iPhone asks for your permission to track. Its section below lists exactly what it sends.
  • Our other apps have no user accounts. Besides Egg Island, only the Android versions of Rabese (analytics and crash reports) and Slomoly (analytics) use Firebase; their sections below list what they send.
  • We do not collect personal information such as your name, email address, phone number, contacts or location. The only thing you type that leaves your device is the Egg Island leaderboard name you choose.
  • Egg Island (iPhone and Android) and the Android versions of Elight Studio, Slomoly and Rabese show ads from Google AdMob. Google collects some device information to show and measure ads, as described in section 4. Maneki Dict, Simple Reader and the iPhone versions of Slomoly and Rabese show no ads.

2. Information we collect

Apart from what Egg Island sends (section 3), we do not collect personal information. Content and settings are stored only on your device, in each app's private storage or in folders you can see (such as your music, movies or photos). You can delete them in the app, with your device's file or photo manager, or by uninstalling the app. If you choose to share a file with another app (for example a messaging app), that app's privacy policy applies.

3. App by app

Elight Studio (Android)

  • Microphone: used only while you record (in Record, in the Studio, and when you measure recording latency). Recordings are saved on your device.
  • Music and audio files: files you choose are copied into the app's private storage and processed on your device. Results are saved in the Music/ElightLab folder.
  • Songs, MIDI and SoundFonts: Studio projects, notes from a connected MIDI keyboard and SoundFont files you load are stored in the app's private storage.
  • Ads: a banner on the main screens and, at most once each time you open the app, a full-screen ad after you save something. Ads are never shown while you record or play in the Studio. See section 4.

Slomoly (iPhone and Android)

  • Your videos: videos you record or import are stored only on your device, inside the app. Editing, rendering and exporting happen on your device.
  • Camera and microphone: used to record video and sound, only while you record.
  • Photos: on iPhone, add-only access saves exported videos to your library; Slomoly cannot read your library. On Android, exports are saved to Movies/Slomoly. Videos and songs you import are the ones you choose in the system picker.
  • iPhone: no ads, no tracking across apps or websites, and no third-party advertising, analytics or crash-reporting SDKs.
  • Android: supported by ads from Google AdMob: a banner on the home screen, and an optional rewarded video you can watch to export without the watermark. See section 4.
  • Analytics (Android): Firebase Analytics records how the app is used (sessions, screens and tools opened, exports started and finished, ad events) under an ID that Firebase generates, with the device model, Android version and an approximate location derived from the IP address. It never includes your videos, songs or project contents, does not use the advertising ID and is not used for ad personalization. In the EEA and UK it starts only after the consent step and follows your ad consent choices.
  • Deleting a project deletes its files. Exports saved to your photos stay there until you delete them.

Rabese (iPhone and Android)

  • iPhone: no ads, no tracking, no advertising identifier (IDFA), no third-party analytics, no location.
  • Android: supported by ads from Google AdMob: ad cards labelled "Ad" inside long sentence lists, a banner on the study-card and quiz screens, and a full-screen ad after you finish a study round or quiz (at most once every few minutes). Full-screen ads are never shown in the middle of a lesson or while pronunciation audio plays. In the EEA and UK you are asked for consent first. See section 4.
  • Analytics and crash reports (Android): Firebase Analytics records how the app is used (sessions, screens opened, ad events) under an ID that Firebase generates, with the device model, Android version and an approximate location derived from the IP address. It starts only after the consent step and follows your ad consent choices. Firebase Crashlytics sends crash reports (device model, Android version, where in the code the crash happened). Neither includes your bookmarks, progress or searches.
  • On-device data: bookmarks, sentences you marked as learned and when, test counts, recent searches and settings (language, pinyin, sound, haptics, daily goal) are stored in a local database, never synced and excluded from cloud backup. Delete them in Settings → Reset learning data, or by uninstalling the app.
  • Offline content: all sentences and audio ship inside the app; your learning data is never sent anywhere. The Android version connects to the internet only to load ads and send the analytics and crash reports described above. Links (policy, support, content sources) open in your browser only when you tap them.
  • Saving images: only when you choose to save your progress card. iPhone asks for add-only Photos access; Android 8–9 asks for storage write access; Android 10 and later need no permission. The app never reads your existing photos.
  • Purchases: the current version has none. If paid features are offered, Apple or Google handles the payment; we never see your card details or store account.
  • Example sentences come from a community source (Tatoeba) and are filtered for sensitive content, but may mildly mention real-world topics such as alcohol, news-style violence or medicine.

Maneki Dict (Android)

  • Offline dictionary, no internet: the Japanese–Vietnamese dictionary ships inside the app and the app does not request internet access. Searches never leave your device.
  • No ads, no tracking: no advertising ID, no third-party analytics or crash-reporting SDKs, no location.
  • On-device data: your recent searches, favorite words, vocabulary cards (name, description and words), display name and settings (number of suggestions) are stored only in the app's private storage. Delete them in the app (history, favorites, cards) or by clearing the app's data or uninstalling it. If Android device backup is turned on, Android may include this data in your own Google account backup; we cannot access it.
  • Profile picture: optional. You pick an image in the system picker; only that image is copied into the app's private storage. The app never reads your other photos and needs no storage permission.
  • Home screen widget: shows your recent searches on your home screen if you add it.
  • Feedback and sharing: "Send feedback" opens your email app addressed to us, and "Share app" opens Android's share sheet. Nothing is sent unless you send it yourself.
  • Permissions: only vibration (for touch feedback).

Simple Reader (Android)

  • Your books: documents you pick in the Android file picker, or open from another app with "Open with" or "Share", are copied into the app's own storage on your device (Android/data/com.delight.ereader/files/Books) and opened and rendered on your device. They are never uploaded. Remove them with "Delete book" in the app, by clearing the app's data or by uninstalling it.
  • On-device data: recent books and their cover thumbnails, the last page you read, bookmarks, per-book view settings (zoom, crop, night mode) and app settings are stored only in the app's private storage. Settings backups, if you create them, also stay on your device. If Android device backup is turned on, Android may include this data in your own Google account backup; we cannot access it.
  • Internet: the app has no servers of its own. It connects to the internet only when you open a document from a web link (http/https) or a network share (SMB) yourself; the file is downloaded directly from that address. Books you open from your phone are read offline.
  • No ads, no tracking: no advertising ID, no third-party analytics or crash-reporting SDKs, no location.
  • Permissions: internet access (above). On Android 12 and earlier the app may ask to read storage so it can reopen books saved by older versions; you can decline and still use every feature. On Android 9 and earlier it may also ask to write storage when you copy, move or rename a book. Android 13 and later need no storage permission.
  • Feedback and sharing: "Send feedback" opens your email app addressed to us, "Share app" opens Android's share sheet, and our Facebook page opens in your browser or the Facebook app. Nothing is sent unless you send it yourself.

Egg Island (iPhone and Android)

  • On-device data: game progress, in-game currency, pets, quests and settings are stored on your device. We keep no server backup, so clearing the app's data or uninstalling it removes them for good.
  • Leaderboard: the game signs in anonymously (no email, phone or password) and sends a random player ID, the display name you choose, your scores and technical data about each run to our leaderboard database, hosted by Supabase. Other players can see your display name and scores. The data is used to show rankings, check scores and limit cheating. Delete it in Settings → Privacy → Leaderboard data, or see the data deletion page.
  • Analytics and crash reports: Firebase Analytics records how the game is played (runs and scores, screens visited, pet upgrades, tutorial steps, ad and purchase events) under an ID that Firebase generates, plus a few grouped profile values such as the range of runs played. Firebase Crashlytics sends crash reports. These never include your display name, email, phone number or precise location. On iPhone they are sent only if you allow tracking (next point).
  • Tracking on iPhone: after the ad consent step, the game shows Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt. If you choose Allow, AdMob may use your advertising identifier (IDFA) to show personalized ads, and Google may link your game activity with data from other companies' apps and websites (Google signals). If you choose Ask App Not to Track, the IDFA is not used, ads are not personalized, and analytics and crash reports are turned off. You can change this at any time in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • Ads: optional rewarded ads you choose to watch for in-game rewards, and full-screen ads between runs, from Google AdMob. Where the law requires it, you are asked for consent first and can change your choice in Settings → Privacy → Ad privacy options. See section 4.
  • Purchases: optional diamond packs. Apple or Google handles the payment; RevenueCat receives the purchase receipt and an anonymous purchaser ID so the diamonds can be delivered. We never see your card details or store account.
  • Age: Egg Island is meant for players aged 13 and older.

4. Advertising (Google AdMob)

This section applies to Egg Island (iPhone and Android) and to Elight Studio, Slomoly and Rabese for Android. We do not collect data for ads ourselves. To show, limit and measure ads and to prevent fraud, Google's Mobile Ads SDK collects and processes:

  • your device's advertising ID (on iPhone, only if you allow tracking in Egg Island),
  • your IP address and the approximate location derived from it,
  • device information (model, operating system, language, network type) and app information,
  • ad interactions (impressions, clicks) and diagnostic data such as crash logs.

This information is handled by Google under its own policies:

  • Google Mobile Ads SDK data disclosure
  • How Google uses information from apps that use its services
  • Google Privacy Policy

5. Your choices

  • Consent. Where the law requires it (for example in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, Switzerland and some US states), the app asks for your choice before ads are requested. You can choose "Do not consent" and still use every feature, and change your choice at any time in the app: Settings, Ad privacy choices.
  • Advertising ID. On Android you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings (Settings, Google, Ads, or Settings, Privacy, Ads). Without it, ads are not personalized.
  • Tracking on iPhone. Egg Island asks before tracking. You can change your answer at any time in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking, or turn off Allow Apps to Request to Track for all apps.
  • Egg Island leaderboard. Delete your player ID, display name and scores in Settings → Privacy → Leaderboard data, or by email (section 11).
  • Opt-out tools. You can also manage interest-based advertising at adssettings.google.com and youradchoices.com.
  • Permissions. You can allow or revoke microphone, camera and photo access at any time in your device settings.

6. Links to other websites and services

Ads and links in our apps may lead to third-party websites or apps. Information you provide there is governed by that party's privacy policy and terms, not by this policy. We do not control and are not responsible for third-party websites or services.

7. Children

Our apps are not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through one of our apps, please contact us and we will help remove it.

8. Security

Your content stays on your device and is protected by your device's security. Data sent for advertising, analytics, the Egg Island leaderboard and purchases is encrypted in transit. These providers may process data outside your country under their own data protection safeguards.

9. Legal requirements

We may disclose information if required by law, to respond to legal process, to protect our rights or property, or to investigate fraud, illegal activity or threats to anyone's safety. Apart from Egg Island leaderboard records, we do not hold personal information or your content, so we generally have nothing to disclose.

10. Changes to this policy

If an app starts handling data differently, this page will be updated before that version is released, with a new effective date.
