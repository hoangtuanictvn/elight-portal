How we make apps

A few rules every Elight Labs app follows, from the first build to every update.

Your content stays on your phone

Recordings, videos and documents are processed on the device. Nothing you create is uploaded.

No sign-up to get started

Our tools have no accounts and no analytics. Open the app and start working.

Works without a connection

The dictionary, the reader and the editors keep working offline.

Clear about ads

Some apps are free with ads. Our privacy policy says which ones and what that means.

Questions, feedback or a bug to report?

Write to us about any of our apps. We usually reply within two business days.
