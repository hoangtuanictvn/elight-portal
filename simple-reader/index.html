Simple ReaderPDF reader and DjVu viewer · Android

Read PDF and DjVu, offline.

A small, fast PDF reader and DjVu viewer that works offline.

Get it free on Google Play

Android 7.0 and later · No ads

Small, fast, offline.

Formats

  • PDF and XPS / OXPS
  • DjVu (DJVU, DJV)
  • CBZ comic books
  • FB2 e-books, also zipped (.fb2.zip)

Reading

  • Open books with the Android file picker (Downloads, internal storage, SD card, cloud drives) or from other apps with "Open with" and "Share".
  • Recent books on a bookshelf with cover thumbnails; every book reopens on the last page you read.
  • Continuous scroll or single pages with page-turn animations, night mode, auto and manual margin cropping, splitting two-page scans, zoom, full screen and orientation lock.
  • Table of contents, bookmarks, go to page and text search in PDF documents.
  • Adjustable brightness, tap zones and hardware keys.

Privacy

No account, no ads and no analytics. Books are copied into the app's own storage on your phone and never uploaded. The app connects to the internet only when you open a document from a web link yourself. Details: privacy policy.
