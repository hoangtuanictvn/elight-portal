Look words up fast

Suggestions appear as you type. Search in kanji, hiragana, katakana or romaji (for example "neko", "taberu"). Results favor short words that closely match what you typed, thanks to full-text search (FTS) running right on your phone.

Understand every word

  • Vietnamese meanings grouped by part of speech (noun, verb, adjective…).
  • Kanji in the word: Sino-Vietnamese reading, meaning, On and Kun readings.
  • Example sentences in Japanese with kana readings and Vietnamese translations.

Learn your way

  • Favorites: tap the heart to save words you want to remember.
  • Vocabulary cards: group words by topic (for example "Animals", "JLPT N4"), rename them, add a description, remove words you know.
  • History: see the words you looked up recently, with a home screen widget.
  • Personalize: set a display name, a profile picture and the number of suggestions (10 to 50).

No internet needed

The whole dictionary is built into the app, so you can look words up anywhere, even on a plane or with no signal. The app has no ads and needs no account.
