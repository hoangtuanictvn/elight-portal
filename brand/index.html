One mark, six small apps.

The Elight Labs logo, colors and type, and how to use them.

An E that switches on.

Three rounded bars form the E. They also read as a stack of apps. The dot beside the top bar is the light in Elight: a small signal that something is on.

The mark stays readable down to 16 px, the size of a browser tab icon.

Color

One warm accent on quiet neutrals. Coral is for the mark, links and the main action, never for large blocks of text.

In dark mode the accent lightens to #FF7A57 and the background becomes #0E0E10, so text keeps WCAG AA contrast.

Geist regular for body text, at a comfortable 1.55 line height. One family across the whole site, self-hosted, with the system font as a fallback.
