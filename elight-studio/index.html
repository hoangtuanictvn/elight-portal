Record, write, beat, mix.

Everything you need to sketch a song, with the audio tools you already know.

Write melodies in a piano roll

Play the on-screen keys or a USB or Bluetooth MIDI keyboard, then move, resize and quantize notes.

Make beats on drum pads

A synthesized kit with kick, snare, hats, toms and more. Or start a new song on a ready-made beat.

Mix with effects

EQ with low cut, compressor, reverb and tempo-synced delay on every track, and a master limiter.

Audio tools, still here

Cut an MP3 on its waveform for a ringtone, record your voice, convert between MP3, AAC, WAV and FLAC, merge clips, change speed and pitch, or layer two tracks.
