How can we help?

Answers to common questions about recording, instruments, exporting and the audio tools.

My recordings are a little late against the beat. How do I fix it?

Open a song in Studio and choose Recording latency from the menu. Tap Measure automatically: the app plays a few clicks through the speaker and listens for them, so turn the volume up, unplug headphones and keep quiet for 6 seconds. You can also set the offset by hand.

Can I hear myself while recording?

Yes, when headphones are connected. Without headphones monitoring stays off so the speaker doesn't feed back into the microphone.

How do I use a MIDI keyboard?

Connect a USB or Bluetooth MIDI keyboard, add an instrument track and play. Arm recording to capture the notes, then edit them in the piano roll.

Where are my files saved?

Songs and Studio projects stay inside the app. Exported songs and the results of the audio tools are saved in the Music/ElightLab folder on your phone and appear in the app's Library, where you can play, rename, share or delete them.

Which formats can I open and export?

The audio tools open MP3, M4A, WAV and FLAC files. Songs and tool results export as MP3, AAC, WAV or FLAC.

How do I change my ad privacy settings?

Where the law requires a choice, open Settings in Elight Studio and tap Ad privacy choices. You can also reset your advertising ID in your phone's Google settings.

How do I change the app language?

Open Settings in Elight Studio and tap Language: English, Tiếng Việt, 简体中文, Español, 日本語 and 한국어.
